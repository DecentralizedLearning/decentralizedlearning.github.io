Under the hood

Local learning.
Global progress.

We try to solve a shared optimization problem across a network of devices without a central view of their data.

  1. 01

    A network of devices

    \(n\) devices, indexed by \(i \in \{1, \dots, n\}\), form a communication graph \(G = (V, E)\). Each device exchanges information only with its neighbors \(N_i = \{ j : (i, j) \in E \}\).

  2. 02

    The model parameters

    \(W \in \mathbb{R}^d\) represents the model parameters.

  3. 03

    Private, heterogeneous data

    \(D_i\) is the local data distribution at device \(i\). It is private and never leaves the device. Different devices can have different distributions (non-IID data).

  4. 04

    A local objective

    \[ F_i(W; D_i) = \mathbb{E}_{\xi \sim D_i} \big[ \ell(W; \xi) \big] \]

    The local objective at device \(i\), where \(\ell(W; \xi)\) is the loss of model \(W\) on sample \(\xi\).

  5. 05

    The global objective

    \(f(W)\) is the average of the local objectives. Device \(i\) can evaluate (stochastic) gradients of \(F_i\) only, never of \(f\).
